About Me

Full Stack Developer working across frontend, backend, APIs and databases, with a growing focus on AI integration and automation.

Full Stack Developer · AI & Automation

Hi, I'm Ipshita Sett — I build web applications end to end, and add AI and automation where they make the product better.

  • Full Stack Development
  • AI Integration
  • Automation

My work covers the whole application: React and Next.js interfaces, Node.js and Express APIs behind them, and MongoDB, PostgreSQL, MSSQL or Firebase underneath.

I started in frontend development, building React interfaces and learning how components, state and routing fit together. Real projects kept pulling me further down the stack: first consuming APIs, then writing them, then handling authentication, databases and server-side logic until I was owning features from the UI to the data layer.

Across 3 years and 15+ projects, that scope has kept widening. I now integrate existing AI models and LLM APIs into applications, connect third-party services, and automate repetitive workflows. I also use AI-assisted tools day to day as part of how I build and ship.
